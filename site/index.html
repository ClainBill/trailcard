What makes it different

Built around how you actually look things up

It reads the trail, not just the page

The thing worth remembering often spans several pages. Your searches and clicks tell it which connection you were after.

Cards with several fields

One card holds the name, the role, the film and the pictures, and every field can be the question or the clue.

You approve every card

Nothing is saved until you've seen it. Edit fields, rewrite questions, or remove what you don't care about.

Modern scheduling

Reviews are timed with FSRS, the algorithm behind current Anki, tuned to a retention target you choose.

Pictures that last

Images are saved with the card, including ones from earlier pages in your trail, so they don't break later.

Free, private AI

Cards are drafted by an AI on your own computer, through Chrome's built-in model or the free Ollama app. A paid Claude key is optional for the sharpest cards.

Privacy

Your history stays yours

An extension that watches your browsing has to earn that. There is no Trailcard server: everything lives in your browser.

  • Stored locally, cleared dailyThe trail never leaves your device on its own and is dropped after 24 hours.
  • Read only when you askYour trail and the current page are read only when you click Create Flashcard. With the free AI they never leave your computer; they reach Anthropic only if you choose Claude and add your own key.
  • Sensitive sites are skippedMail, banking and password managers are blocked by default, and you can add any site to the list.
  • Pause any timeOne switch stops all tracking. Web addresses are trimmed of their query strings before anything is sent.

Get it

Install in a minute

Trailcard is an early build and isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet, so you load it by hand.

Download Trailcard (.zip)
  1. Download the zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Open Settings and follow the free AI setup: it checks what your computer supports and walks you through the free Ollama app if needed.
